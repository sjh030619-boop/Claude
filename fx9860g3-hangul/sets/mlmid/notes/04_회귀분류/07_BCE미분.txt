연관: →4/06 →2/05
1) (h - y)x_{j}
---

BCE 미분 풀이
답: 1) (h-y)x_{j}

dh/dz=h(1-h), dz/dw_{j}=x_{j}
곱하면 (h-y)x_{j}
---
